## Data contracts
Canonical bar: {start,end,open,high,low,close,volume}; UTC seconds, positive finite consistent OHLC, nonnegative volume, ordered nonoverlapping completed periods. Symbol identity includes provider, ticker, asset class, currency and contract/proxy status. Yahoo equity/crypto/continuous-futures bars retain their own identities; do not apply spot TradingView levels to futures prices. Treat futures roll history as exploratory, not executable contract sizing.

entrydesk.factors.build_factors(bars_1h, bars_15m, micro=None) -> dict: trend_momentum, volatility_channel, volume_profile, microstructure, volume_money_flow, smart_money_derivatives, composite_alpha_score, signal_recommendation, coverage. ATR/ADX calculated causally; Astra MACD/RSI/VWAP/scoring retained with documented fixes. VWAP uses actual supplied interval/window and is labeled; no fabricated 24-hour evidence from daily candles. Coverage lists unavailable tiers. Reject malformed candles before any permissive upstream helper sees them.

entrydesk.data.collect_symbol(symbol, asset_class, now) -> dict: {symbol,asset_class,source,currency,contract_kind,bars_1h,bars_15m,quote,market_open,errors}; quote {price,time,bid,ask} may be null. Only actual observation time, never download time, supports quote freshness. Defaults: stocks SPY,NVDA,AMD,AVGO,ASML,TSM,MU,INTC,QCOM,AMAT,LRCX,KLAC,ARM; crypto BTC-USD,ETH-USD; commodities GC=F,SI=F,CL=F. Bounded fetches; preserve old data on failures but clearly stale, never fresh-ready from cache. Historical data public, no credentials. Intraday requests hourly; no claim of scalping latency.

entrydesk.signals.evaluate(bundle, now, validation=None) -> dict: {symbol,asset_class,source,as_of,state,side,entry,stop,target,net_rr,score,factors,reasons,validation}. Warmup >=60 completed 1h candles; 20-bar price breakout + volume>=1.5 preceding median + direction agrees with Astra recommendation. 15m closes confirm breakout. Stop based on prior ATR, target >=2R after modeled costs; reject bad geometry, spread, missing/stale quotes, market closure, mismatched sources, missing future contract economics and failed validation. States WATCHING/CANDIDATE/BLOCKED/PAPER_READY; no executable orders. Candidate is a price pattern, not validated success probability. Paper readiness requires holdout validation plus forward-paper evidence, never merely a high score.

entrydesk.validation.backtest(bars,asset_class) -> dict: fixed-rule chronological simulation, next-bar fills only, position overlap prohibited, entry/exit costs, gap-aware stops, stop-first ambiguous candles, timed exits, trade log, drawdown, expectancy, profit factor and separate chronological train/holdout results. Historical evaluation uses only past factor/bar information and omits present-day derivatives/depth. Minimum30 closed holdout trades, positive net expectancy/profit factor>1.1/drawdown<=20% and >=30 forward paper closes required for readiness. Thresholds are release evidence gates, not optimized parameters or guarantees. Insufficient data fails closed. Include bootstrap confidence interval on mean net R if feasible and preserve out-of-sample holdout without tuning to it. Commodity continuous-future results always exploratory until dated contracts/roll/point-value and trading costs exist.

entrydesk CLI collect writes docs/entries.json with {version,generated_at,candidates,validation,status,errors}; private histories under .screener/entrydesk. Demo is explicit synthetic, never fallback into live. Combined app provides asset/state filters, price levels, factor details, veto reasons and validation summary. Existing technical analysis, backtests, research and original breakout remain accessible.
